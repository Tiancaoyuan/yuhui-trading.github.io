About Yuhui Trading

Yuhui Trading (Yuhui Furniture) is a China-based B2B supplier specializing in banquet chairs, hotel seating, and event furniture. We work directly with manufacturers in Hebei and Shandong to provide durable, stackable, competitively priced chairs for global buyers.

Business Model

  • Type: B2B inquiry-based supply (no platform store, direct factory sourcing)
  • Process: Customer inquiry → Quotation → Proforma Invoice → T/T 30% deposit → Production → Balance against B/L copy → Shipment
  • Payment: T/T (bank wire transfer)
  • Lead time: 15–25 days after deposit confirmation
  • Shipping terms: FOB Qingdao / Ningbo, EXW available, CIF on request

Product Range

  • Banquet chairs (classic, Chiavari, Tiffany styles)
  • Folding event chairs
  • Stackable conference / training chairs
  • Hotel dining chairs
  • Custom color & logo service available

Target Markets

Middle East, Europe, UK, US, Australia — event rental companies, hotel procurement, wedding planners, and furniture wholesalers.

Why Choose Us

  • Direct factory pricing, no middleman markup
  • Flexible MOQ for small-to-medium B2B orders
  • Customization: frame color, seat material, logo printing
  • Responsive communication via WhatsApp and email
