About Yuhui Trading

Yuhui Trading (Yuhui Furniture) is an independent online store specializing in banquet chairs, hotel seating, and event furniture. We list our products directly on this site, take orders via WhatsApp and email, and arrange worldwide shipping for our customers.

How We Work

  • Type: Direct-to-customer (DTC) independent store — no platform fees, direct communication
  • Order process: Browse products → Contact us via WhatsApp/email → Confirm model & quantity → Receive invoice → Make payment → We ship
  • Payment: Bank transfer / wire transfer accepted; card payment available through our payment provider
  • Shipping: Worldwide delivery, FOB Qingdao / Ningbo available, EXW on request
  • Lead time: 15–25 days for production and preparation

Product Range

  • Banquet chairs (classic, Chiavari, Tiffany styles)
  • Folding event chairs
  • Stackable conference / training chairs
  • Hotel dining chairs
  • Custom color & logo service available

Target Markets

We ship to customers worldwide — Middle East, Europe, UK, US, Australia and more. Whether you're planning a wedding, furnishing a small venue, or stocking up for an event, we're here to help.

Why Choose Us

  • Direct factory pricing, no middleman markup
  • Flexible order quantities for small businesses and individual buyers
  • Customization: frame color, seat material, logo printing
  • Responsive communication via WhatsApp and email
